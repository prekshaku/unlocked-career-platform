# UnlockED – AI-Powered Career Guidance Platform

## 1. Project Overview

UnlockED is an AI-powered platform designed to help students connect career discovery, skill development, personalized learning resources, and job opportunities in one place.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students often struggle to identify their career goals, understand industry-required skills, find relevant learning resources, and discover suitable internship and job opportunities.

Existing platforms provide these services separately, making it difficult for students to follow a clear career path.

## 3. Proposed Solution

UnlockED provides a personalized career guidance system that helps students:

- Discover suitable career paths.
- Assess their existing skills.
- Identify skill gaps.
- Receive personalized learning recommendations.
- Follow structured career roadmaps.
- Track learning progress.
- Explore internships and job opportunities.

## 4. Core Modules

1. User Registration and Profile Management
2. Career Interest Assessment
3. Skill Gap Analysis
4. AI-Powered Recommendations
5. Personalized Learning Roadmap
6. Progress Tracking
7. Internship and Job Opportunities

## 6. System Workflow

Student Registration
→ Career Assessment
→ Skill Gap Identification
→ AI Recommendations
→ Personalized Learning Roadmap
→ Progress Tracking
→ Internship and Job Opportunities

## 7. Initial MVP Scope

The 24-hour MVP will focus on:

- Student profile creation.
- Basic career interest assessment.
- Skill gap identification.
- Personalized learning recommendations.
- Career roadmap generation.
- Relevant learning resources.

## 8. Future Enhancements

- Regional language support.
- Additional career domains.

- More learning platform integrations.
- Expanded internship and recruitment partnerships.
